Privacy policy

Draft, 9 September 2026. The plain-language version. It has to match the formal policy posted at the App Store link, and a lawyer should check both.

The short version

Nothing is public. Nobody can find you. Your photos are sealed on your phone before they are sent, and the key is destroyed when they expire. We keep as little as we can, and we do not sell, share or analyse your content.

What we collect

  • An email address, to send you a sign-in code.
  • The name and handle you chose.
  • A public key generated on your phone, so friends can seal photos for you. The private half never leaves your phone.
  • The photos, captions, voice notes and replies you post, sealed. We store the sealed bytes and the key wrapped for each recipient. We cannot open them.
  • Who has opened a drop and when, shown only to the person who posted it, and deleted with the drop.
  • Reports you file and accounts you block, so we can act on them.
  • Device tokens for notifications, if you turn them on.

What we do not collect

No advertising identifiers. No analytics on what you post or look at. No location: photo metadata, including GPS, is removed before upload. No contacts list. No behavioural profile.

Who can see what

  • Your photos: only the people you chose, only until the drop expires.
  • Your name, handle and avatar: only people you share a circle with, or have added by code.
  • Your email: only us, for sign-in. Never shown to other members.

How long we keep things

  • A drop: until its expiry, then deleted. Storage also deletes objects automatically after 31 days regardless of anything else.
  • Your account details: until you delete your account.
  • Reports: as long as needed to act on them.

Cookies and tracking

None. The app uses no cookies, no advertising or analytics software, and no third-party code of any kind. Our website sets no cookies either, which is why it shows no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

Where it lives

On servers run by Supabase, in the region chosen for the project. Sealed content is stored in object storage. Access is controlled by rules enforced in the database, not by the app.

Your rights

You can see and change your name and handle in the app. You can delete your account, which deletes your live drops and account details. Where the law gives you further rights (access, portability, correction, complaint to a regulator), write to the address on the profile screen and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Ember is not for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly keep data about them. If you believe we have, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will tell you in the app before a change that matters takes effect.

Who we are

Ember is made by Rabiat Sadiq. Questions, requests about your data, and anything else: rabiat.dev@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days, usually much sooner.
