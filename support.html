support

ember is in betabeta. things will break, and that is the point of you being here.

something broke

write to rabiat.dev@gmail.com. say what you did, what you expected, and what happened. a screenshot helps. fixes usually ship within days and arrive as a card from ember in your feed.

report a photo or a person

every photo has report and block in its menu, the three dots. reported content is hidden from you immediately and reviewed within 24 hours. for anything urgent, email the address above with "report" in the subject.

your account

sign in with your email and password. forgot the password? tap "email me a link instead" on the sign in screen. to delete your account, open settings from the gear on your profile: it removes your live photos and your account details at once, and needs no email to us.

your data

what we keep and why is in the privacy policy. to ask for a copy of your data or have it removed, email the address above; we answer within 30 days.

refunds

ember is free. if a paid plan is ever offered it is sold through the App Store, and Apple handles refunds from your purchase history.

ember is made by Rabiat Sadiq. rabiat.dev@gmail.com. no cookies, no trackers, nothing to consent to.
