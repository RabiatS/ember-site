Draft, 9 September 2026. Written in plain language on purpose. A lawyer should read this before it ships; nothing here is legal advice.

What Ember is

Ember lets you share photos with small groups of friends. Everything you post expires: after the time you choose (a day, three days, a week, or longer on a paid plan) it is deleted and cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

By using Ember you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use it.

Who can use it

You must be 13 or older. Tapping through the first screens is you confirming that. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 13, we close it.

Your account

You sign in with an email address and a code we send to it. Keep your email account secure; anyone who can read your email can sign in as you. You are responsible for what happens on your account.

Your photos are yours

You own what you post. You give Ember a limited licence to store, seal, transmit and display your photos to the people you chose, for as long as the drop lives, and no longer. When a drop expires that licence ends and the content is deleted.

Your friends may keep a photo only if you allowed it on that drop. A kept photo is a copy in their library, outside Ember, and these terms cannot reach it. The same is true of screenshots: we tell you when one is taken; we cannot prevent it.

What you may not post

Anything you do not have the right to share. Anything that shows or targets a person without their consent in a way they would object to. Nudity involving anyone under 18, ever. Threats, harassment, or content that encourages harm to yourself or others. Anything illegal where you or the people you share it with live.

We remove content that breaks these rules and may close the account. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Circles and codes

You can only see a circle you were invited to. Codes expire and are single or limited use. Do not post codes publicly. Leaving a circle removes the drops you posted to it.

What we promise about deletion, and what we do not

Each drop is encrypted under its own key before it leaves your phone. When the drop expires, that key is destroyed and the stored data is deleted. Anything that survives, in a cache or a backup, is unreadable without the key.

We cannot delete copies that left the system through a keep, a screenshot, a photo of a screen, or any other means. Ember reduces how long photos exist; it cannot make a shared photo impossible to copy.

Ending your account

You can delete your account from the profile screen at any time. Your live drops are deleted immediately and their keys destroyed. We may suspend or close accounts that break these terms.
