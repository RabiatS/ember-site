photos for small circles.

nothing here is forever. share photos with the people you actually mean, and watch them go when they go. no followers, no feed to be found on, no forever.

small rooms, not an audience

you post to a circle: a handful of friends, invited by a six-letter code read across a table. there is no follower count, no search, no public profile.

you can watch it go

every photo has a clock on it. a day, three days, a week. it loses resolution as it goes, so you can see it leaving and keep the one worth keeping. when the clock runs out the key is destroyed, and the photo is gone on every phone and on ours.

sealed before it leaves your phone

each photo is encrypted on your phone under its own key, wrapped for each friend's phone. the server holds bytes it cannot open.

ember is in beta on iPhonebeta. it is invite only: a friend who has it lets you in.
